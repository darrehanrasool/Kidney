# Known Limitations & Bias Analysis

1. **Retrospective Cohort Bias**: Benchmark evaluations rely predominantly on historical, retrospective clinical registries. Prospective multi-site validation is required before bedside deployment.
2. **Missing Feature Sensitivity**: Although robust imputation (MICE) is implemented, missing critical biomarkers like serum creatinine or urine albumin degrades diagnostic confidence.
3. **Demographic Representation**: Generalizability across diverse pediatric cohorts or specific ethnic demographics requires targeted calibration.
